Solution

Upload an invoice. Receive 90 % within the hour.

How it works

Three steps, built on open banking.

  1. ReadExtracts amount, buyer and due date from the invoice.
  2. ScoreRates the buyer from payment data the customer shares.
  3. PaySends 90 % now and the rest, minus the fee, when the buyer pays.
