Team Mise · FoodHack 2026 · Challenge 02

Canteens throw away 1 in 4 meals they cook.

Leftover tells the kitchen tonight how many people eat tomorrow.

Problem

Kitchens plan with gut feeling.

  • 180 kg of food wasted per canteen and week.
  • Menus are fixed two weeks ahead, guests decide on the day.
  • Cooks would rather run out than waste, so they cook more.

Solution

A forecast the chef can read in ten seconds.

Leftover combines bookings, weather and holidays into one number per dish, printed on the kitchen tablet each afternoon.

How it works

Three steps, no new hardware.

  1. ReadImports the till data the canteen already has.
  2. PredictA small model learns each dish and weekday.
  3. TellOne number per dish at 3 p.m., on the kitchen tablet.
