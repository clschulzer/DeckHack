Team Servo · RoboHack 2026 · Challenge 05

Warehouse pickers walk 14 km every shift.

Shelfbot brings the shelf to the picker instead.

Problem

Most of a picker's day is walking, not picking.

  • 60 % of shift time is spent between shelves.
  • Full automation costs millions and a new building.
  • Small warehouses cannot afford either.

Solution

A low robot that slides under existing shelves.

Shelfbot lifts a standard shelf, drives it to the packing station and returns it. No rebuild, no new racks, installed in a weekend.

Demo

Prototype run: order in, shelf out in 50 seconds.

Replace this frame with a video of the robot or your live prototype.

How it works

Sense, plan, act. On a €300 bill of materials.

  1. SenseFloor markers and a depth camera locate every shelf.
  2. PlanA fleet planner picks the nearest free robot and path.
  3. ActThe robot lifts the shelf and drives it to the picker.
